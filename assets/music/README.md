# Music library

The editor now reads music from the repository-root `dramas/` folder, not this legacy folder.

Place your own or properly licensed tracks in `dramas/` or a mood subfolder such as `dramas/comedy/`, `dramas/romantic/`, `dramas/emotional/`, `dramas/suspense/`, or `dramas/action/`.

Use clear mood words in filenames and/or folder names. The editor ranks tracks using those labels plus approximate audio energy and brightness. It will keep the original video audio if no confident match is found rather than choosing an unrelated track.

Audio-feature analysis does not understand lyrics or story meaning. Use clear mood labels and only music you own or are licensed to use.

Supported formats: MP3, WAV, M4A and AAC.
